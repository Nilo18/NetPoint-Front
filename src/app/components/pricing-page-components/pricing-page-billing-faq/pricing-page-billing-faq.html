Billing FAQ

Questions before you choose?

Here are the essentials about plans, billing periods, and changing your subscription.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Owners can change plans from Billing & Plans in Settings as their catalog, reporting, or team needs change.

What happens when I choose yearly billing?

The pricing cards show the twelve-month total for the selected plan so the full yearly commitment remains clear.

Is the custom product schema available on Starter?

Yes. Custom product attributes are part of every plan; plan differences are based on catalog limits, team size, reporting, and audit retention.

Which plan includes the full audit suite?

Business Plus includes the full audit suite and 90-day activity-log retention. Professional retains activity for 30 days, while Starter retains it for 7 days.
