Built into NetPoint

Practical controls for everyday security

Strong security should support your team without slowing it down. NetPoint keeps essential controls close to the workflows you use every day.

Secure authentication

Account sign-in and protected routes help ensure that business tools are available only to authenticated users.

Team control

Roles and permissions

Assign access according to responsibility, helping team members see and manage only what their role requires.

Audit visibility

Audit logs provide a clearer record of important actions, helping administrators review changes and maintain accountability.

Business separation

Business settings, users, inventory, and reporting are organized within the appropriate account context.
